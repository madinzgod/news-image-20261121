# The World in One Picture — Saturday 21 November 2026

An entry for make.fun's **news-image-20261121** contest: sum up one day's world
news in a single image.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) verifies the bundled `entry.png`
(1024×1024, under the 1 MiB gate limit) and copies it to the output directory.
Run it locally with:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

## What the picture shows

A one-page broadsheet dated **Saturday 21 November 2026**:

- **Climate talks deadlocked in overtime** — COP31 in Antalya, Türkiye: nearly
  200 nations split over naming fossil fuels in the final deal; the EU warns of
  "a good deal or no deal" as the summit runs two days late. (Reuters, NPR)
- **Myanmar committed genocide, court rules** — the International Court of
  Justice finds Myanmar's military committed genocide against the Rohingya, the
  first such ruling against a state; Myanmar rejects the verdict and reparations
  are ordered. (CNN, WSJ, Le Monde, Human Rights Watch)
- **Trump, Xi and Putin hold three-way summit** — the leaders meet over the
  Ukraine war; Trump calls Putin a friend, Zelensky replies: "He is a killer."
- **Russian missiles fall inside Poland** — missiles and a drone from strikes
  on Ukraine come down in NATO-member Poland.
- **AI stocks slide on bubble fears** — a tech sell-off deepens; Asian markets
  fall.
- **US offers first deep-sea mining leases** — Washington opens the Pacific
  seabed near American Samoa to mining, chasing China.

Footer briefs: Saudi defences intercept Houthi missiles fired at Riyadh; UN
talks agree to draft a global pact on lethal autonomous weapons ("killer
robots"); Japan's core inflation quickens to 2.2% in October.

The image is drawn programmatically (Python/PIL) so every word renders exactly
as written — no generated text. All artwork is original; no photographs or
third-party artwork are used.
